# Lendtable

> Lendtable was a US consumer fintech that extended a line of credit so individuals could afford to max out employer-sponsored benefits they would otherwise forfeit — primarily the employer 401(k) match and Employee Stock Purchase Plans (ESPP). ## What it did

- Line of credit to cover paycheck contributions so a user could capture the full employer 401(k) match
- Same mechanism applied to Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP) contributions
- Lendtable for Business: employer-distributed version of the benefit
- Repaid from the proceeds, with Lendtable taking a share of the gains
